## Customer

- elderly clients
- families
- assisted-living residents

## Offer

- consented intake and scheduling
- companion visits
- light-errand support
- reporting

## Revenue

- per-visit fee
- monthly companion-service platform fee
- reporting package

## Trust Controls

- no lifting/transfer assist without governor gate
- medication reminders always require human sign-off
- service records are auditable, not editable
